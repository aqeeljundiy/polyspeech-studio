# Polyspeech Reels: 3 scripts, no voiceover, paced to be read

## Pacing rules (applied to every beat below)

- **One idea per beat.** Each beat is 6–9s, so a 60s Reel has 7–8 beats, not 15.
- **Captions are 7 words or fewer**, held for 1.5s + 0.4s per word (a 5-word caption gets about 3.5s), and they never change while the UI is doing something important.
- **UI actions breathe:** the screen settles for 1s, then the tap, then the result animates in about 0.6s, then holds for at least 2.5s (+0.3s per word of UI text that matters).
- **Zoom on what matters** so app text appears large on screen. The camera moves between beats, not during reading.
- **Transitions take 0.6–0.8s** and land on a calm frame.
- **Sound:** music + soft UI sounds. No narrator. *Optional:* the app's own tutor voice speaking the target language, because "it talks out loud" is a feature.

Caption timing below is shown as *(words → hold)*.

## Script A: "The trip" · Mandarin · traveler · 60s

*Angle: a deadline and one concrete goal, with the app aimed straight at it.*

| # | Time | What's on screen | Caption | Why it reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–6 | A boarding pass flips in: **Shanghai · departs in 14 days**. The number counts down and settles. | "14 days until Shanghai." *(4 → 3.1s)* | Calm hold after the flip. |
| 2 | 6–12 | A waiter's bubble: 欢迎光临！几位？ with "Welcome! How many?" underneath. The empty reply bubble blinks. | "And I can't even order dinner." *(6 → 3.9s)* | One problem, one image. |
| 3 | 12–21 | Home → **New plan** → types "Order dinner in Shanghai" → **Plan it for me** → the 6 steps drop in one by one, then hold. | "Tell it your goal." (12–15.5) · "It plans every step." (17–21) | Typing is slow enough to read; the plan holds 3s. |
| 4 | 21–29 | Zoom on the lesson card: PHRASE, **我要这个** with pinyin under each word, highlighted word by word, then "I want this one." | "Learn the words you'll actually need." *(6 → 3.9s)* | Card fills the frame; 3s hold after the highlight. |
| 5 | 29–36 | Tap 这个 → the word sheet slides up: **this one**, tones (Tone 4 · falling, Tone 0 · light and short), *Hear it slowly*. | "Every word, explained." *(3 → 2.7s)* | Sheet holds 4s. |
| 6 | 36–45 | Roleplay "Order at a Restaurant", the waiter asks. Tap **?** → "Need help replying?" → English "Can I get the dumplings?" → **SUGGESTED REPLY** 我可以要饺子吗？ | "Stuck? Say it in English." (36–40) · "Get the Mandarin back." (41–45) | Two short captions, each with its own moment. |
| 7 | 45–53 | Speaks the next line → **See correction** → zoom on the lime **CORRECT WAY**: 我要一杯茶。 "I'd like a cup of tea." → *Saved*. | "Mistakes fixed on the spot." *(5 → 3.5s)* | The correction holds 4s at full size. |
| 8 | 53–60 | Real restaurant again: the reply fills in confidently, then the end card: mascot, **Polyspeech**, "Start speaking free". | "Day 14. Dinner, ordered." (53–56.5) | The end card holds 3.5s. |

**Shows:** Plans, Lessons, word sheet, Roleplay, Need help replying, corrections, save.

## Script B: "Grandma's language" · Korean · heritage learner · 60s

*Angle: emotional. He understands his family but can't answer them, and the app closes that gap.*

| # | Time | What's on screen | Caption | Why it reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–7 | A message from 할머니 (Grandma): 밥은 먹었니? ("Have you eaten?"). His reply comes in English: "yeah grandma, I ate!" | "Grandma texts in Korean." (0.8–4) · "I answer in English." (4.2–7) | Two bubbles, two captions, one each. |
| 2 | 7–12 | Clean title moment, mascot pops in. | "This year, I answer in Korean." *(6 → 3.9s)* | No UI competing. |
| 3 | 12–21 | Chat · Live mode · topic **Family**. The tutor speaks first: a Korean line with romanization and English, word chips like "가족 (gajok) — family". | "A tutor that talks first." (12.5–16) · "About my real life." (17–21) | The tutor message is zoomed and held. |
| 4 | 21–31 | Tap **?** → "Need help replying?" → he says "I call my grandma every Sunday" → **SUGGESTED REPLY** 저는 매주 일요일에 할머니께 전화해요. | "Don't know how to say it?" (21–25) · "Say it in English first." (26.5–31) | The result holds 3.5s. |
| 5 | 31–41 | He tries the next one himself → **See correction**: 밥 먹었어? struck through, chip **POLITENESS**, "With elders, use 진지 드셨어요" → **CORRECT WAY**. | "It even teaches respect." *(4 → 3.1s)* at 34–37.5 | 4s on the correction, zoomed. |
| 6 | 41–48 | Settings → **What your tutor knows about you**. Fact chips appear one at a time: "Grandma lives in Busan" · "Calls every Sunday". | "It remembers what matters to me." *(6 → 3.9s)* | The chips stagger slowly. |
| 7 | 48–55 | A phone call screen: **할머니 · calling…** → connected. His bubble: 할머니, 진지 드셨어요? Hers: 아이고, 우리 손자! ("Oh, my grandson!") | "Sunday night. In Korean." *(4 → 3.1s)* | Emotional payoff with room to breathe. |
| 8 | 55–60 | End card. | — | Holds 4s. |

**Shows:** Live chat, the tutor talking first, word chips, Need help replying, the errors list with an issue chip, CORRECT WAY, tutor memory.

## Script C: "Nobody's listening" · Spanish · shy learner · 56s

*Angle: fear of speaking in front of people. The app lets you build up from listening, to typing, to speaking, with no judgment.*

| # | Time | What's on screen | Caption | Why it reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–6 | A classroom-style bubble: "¿Alguien quiere hablar?" ("Anyone want to speak?"). Silence. The cursor never moves. | "Speaking Spanish in class? Terrifying." *(5 → 3.5s)* | A single image. |
| 2 | 6–12 | A dark night screen, 1:12 AM, the phone glows and Polyspeech opens. | "So I practice where nobody's listening." *(6 → 3.9s)* | Mood change, nothing to read but the caption. |
| 3 | 12–21 | **Find your level**: a spoken check → result card **Level 3 · High beginner** with Fluency / Grammar / Vocabulary / Pronunciation bars filling. | "Two minutes. It finds my level." *(6 → 3.9s)* | The bars fill, then hold 3s. |
| 4 | 21–29 | Lesson card **REPEAT AFTER ME**: "¿Dónde está el baño?" The mode switch sits on **Just listen**, then slides to **Type**, and the typed answer is accepted. | "Start by listening. Then type." *(5 → 3.5s)* | One switch change, slow. |
| 5 | 29–37 | The tutor nudges: "You're typing these right. Ready to say the next one out loud?" → **Try speaking** → the mic turns red → stamp **Got it!** | "When I'm ready, I speak." *(5 → 3.5s)* | The nudge text holds 3s before the tap. |
| 6 | 37–44 | Recap: **LESSON COMPLETE** · "You nailed 3 of 3" · "Every single one! You were on fire today." | "No one judging. Just progress." *(5 → 3.5s)* | The recap holds 4s. |
| 7 | 44–50 | Progress: the day streak ticks to 7 and the **Week Warrior** achievement unlocks. | "A little, every night." *(4 → 3.1s)* | One number, one badge. |
| 8 | 50–56 | End card. | — | Holds 4s. |

**Shows:** placement test, answer modes (Just listen → Type → Speak), the tutor's nudge, the Got it! verdict, lesson recap, streak, achievements.
